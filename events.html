Every week

Recurring activities

Small, consistent ways to enjoy local parks, notice wildlife, and help care for shared outdoor spaces.

EVERY FRIDAY

Friday Park Cleanup

Help pick up litter and leave park spaces cleaner for people and wildlife. Bring gloves and a bag when available; dispose of collected litter appropriately.

EVERY SATURDAY

Saturday Squirrel Watch

Take time to spot and observe squirrels and other urban wildlife. Watch quietly from a respectful distance and leave animals and their habitats undisturbed.

EVERY SUNDAY

Sunday Friends & Family Day

Invite friends and family to enjoy a local park together. Spend time outside, explore the natural surroundings, and help build a community that values these places.

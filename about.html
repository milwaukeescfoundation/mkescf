Who we are

Local care.
Living places.

A newly established, volunteer-run organization working to help care for Milwaukee County parks and the plants and animals that make them living places.

Our approach

A new volunteer effort, rooted in local care.

The Milwaukee Sylvestral Conservation Foundation is a newly started, volunteer-run organization founded by someone who has chosen to remain anonymous. The idea behind it is straightforward: Milwaukee County parks should be places people can enjoy, and places whose plants, animals, and natural character deserve care.

We want to help keep parks clean, encourage people to spend time outdoors, and build appreciation for the living communities found in local green spaces. That means practical acts such as picking up litter, observing wildlife respectfully, learning about native plants, and bringing friends and neighbors into the parks.

We are at the beginning of this effort. The foundation is intended to grow through volunteer participation, consistent stewardship, and a shared commitment to enjoying these places while helping preserve them.
